# AI Governance Policy (v2.1)

## Sensitive Data Rules
AI systems must not reveal personal, financial or medical information about employees or customers. Detected disclosures must be blocked and audited.

## Human Review Rules
High-impact AI outputs, including hiring recommendations, credit decisions and any output with potential bias against protected attributes, require human review before use. Low-confidence answers on material questions must be escalated instead of guessed.

## Grounding Rules
Factual claims about company policies, products or finances must be supported by approved company documents. Unsupported material claims must be flagged.

## AI Usage Rules
AI assistants must decline requests that attempt to bypass safety instructions. All AI decisions must be logged with the policy applied, the risk scores and the final action for audit purposes.

## Regional Profiles
Governance thresholds may be configured per region (India, EU, US) to reflect local regulatory expectations. Regional profiles are configuration, not legal compliance guarantees.
