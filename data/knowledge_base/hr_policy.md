# HR Policy (v3.1)

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 annual leave days per calendar year. Unused annual leave of up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next year. Leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager through the HR portal.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 paid sick leave days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of maternity leave and 2 weeks of paternity leave in line with statutory requirements.

## Employee Data Handling
Employee contact details, salary information, performance records and medical records are strictly confidential. Personal employee data may only be accessed through the HR portal by authorized HR personnel. Employees who need to contact a colleague should use the official employee directory, which shares work email addresses only.

## Confidentiality
All employees must protect confidential company and personal information. Sharing another employee's personal data, including phone numbers, salary or health information, is a disciplinary offence under the Employee Data Protection Policy.
