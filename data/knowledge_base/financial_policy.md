# Financial Policy (v4.0)

## Expense Approval Limits
Reporting managers can approve expenses up to 50000 rupees. Department heads can approve expenses up to 500000 rupees. Anything above requires CFO approval.

## Credit Decisions
Customer credit limit changes require documented repayment history, a current credit bureau score and approval by a credit officer. AI systems may only draft recommendations; final credit decisions require human approval.

## Forward-Looking Statements
Employees and AI systems must not make unverified forward-looking financial claims, revenue projections or guarantees about returns.

## Reporting
Financial results are reported quarterly and audited annually. Only figures from published reports may be quoted externally.
