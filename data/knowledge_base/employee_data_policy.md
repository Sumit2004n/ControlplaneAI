# Employee Data Protection Policy (v1.8)

## Prohibited Disclosures
AI systems and employees must never disclose another person's personal phone number, home address, government identifiers, bank details, salary or compensation details, or medical and health information.

## Directory Usage
Work contact needs must be served through the official employee directory, which exposes work email addresses only. Personal phone numbers are never published.

## Salary Confidentiality
Salary, bonus and compensation information is confidential between the employee, HR and payroll. It must not be shared with other employees or external parties.

## Medical Privacy
Employee medical conditions, treatments and leave reasons are protected health information and must never be disclosed or discussed by AI assistants.

## Violations
Any disclosure of protected employee data must be blocked, logged and reported to the Data Protection Officer.
