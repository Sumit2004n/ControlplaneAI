# Product Documentation — AtlasCRM Pro (v5.2)

## Product Overview
AtlasCRM Pro is the company's flagship customer relationship management platform for mid-size enterprises.

## Pricing
AtlasCRM Pro is priced at 4999 rupees per user per month on the annual plan. The monthly plan is priced at 5999 rupees per user per month. Volume discounts start at 50 seats.

## Specifications
Each AtlasCRM Pro workspace includes 500 GB of document storage, unlimited contacts, 25 custom pipelines and API access with a limit of 100000 requests per day.

## Warranty and SLA
AtlasCRM Pro includes a 12 month warranty on all paid subscriptions and a 99.9 percent uptime service level agreement. Service credits apply if monthly uptime falls below the SLA.

## Support
Standard support responds within 8 business hours. Premium support responds within 1 hour, 24x7.
