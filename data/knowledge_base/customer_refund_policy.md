# Customer Refund Policy (v2.4)

## Refund Period
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval.

## Cancellation Policy
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 24 hours of placing the order. After 24 hours, a cancellation fee of 5 percent applies until the order is shipped.

## Non-Refundable Items
Customized products, downloadable software licenses and gift cards are non-refundable.

## Escalation Process
Unresolved refund complaints are escalated from Level 1 support to a Level 2 specialist within 48 hours. If still unresolved after 10 business days, the case is escalated to the customer ombudsman team.

## Customer Data
Support agents must never disclose a customer's payment details, bank account information or personal contact details in chat or email.
